## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

The game includes free-roam café sections during work shifts. Move around the coffeehouse with the keyboard, mouse, compatible controller, or touchscreen. Use Freeze Time when exploring the café or visiting the upstairs lounge and character rooms.

### Tools & Inventory

Stocking supplies is part of the café management loop. Enable Unlimited Supplies before purchasing or using ingredients if you want to prepare food and drinks without resource limits. Infinite Café Funds can be used for regular purchases and café upgrades.

### World & Environment

Whiskers Coffeehouse is the central location. During the day, manage stocking, cooking, serving, work shifts, and improvements. At night, spend time in the lounge or visit staff rooms to advance personal relationships and unlock character-specific events.

### Combat & Weapons

The game has no combat system. Progress is based on café management, dialogue choices, free-roam activities, relationship development, minigames, exploration, and story events.
